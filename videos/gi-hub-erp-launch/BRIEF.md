## Intent

A 30-second product launch promo for **GI Hub ERP v1.2.0**, an internal
multi-site warehouse inventory and procurement ERP built for General Industries.
The audience is management and prospective internal users — people who already
know what a warehouse system is and do not need the category explained.

The video is a feature reveal, not a demo. It is typographic and abstract: no
screen recordings, no people, no stock photography. The product's own character
is engineering precision, so the video should feel like that — confident,
declarative, unhurried in its language even though the cutting is quick.

Sleek dark-mode tech aesthetic: near-black canvas, high-contrast white type, a
gold primary accent and a cool cyan secondary. Thin hairline rules and faint
grid structure. Modern geometric sans-serif, tight tracking on headlines.

Motion is kinetic typography throughout — words mask-reveal from below on a
staggered per-word beat, numbers count up, cuts are quick wipes or
scale-throughs rather than fades. Easing should feel expensive: slow out, fast
settle. Nothing holds still for more than about two seconds.

## Assets

No captured assets. There is no public marketing site for this product — it is
an internal system — so this runs the no-capture path. Every claim in the video
comes from the repository itself and is verifiable there.

## Customizations

- Count-up animation on the number **126** in the access-control beat.
- Two-tier bar motif in the estimator beat: one tier gold (stock available now),
  one tier amber (stock still on order). The two tiers must read as visually
  separate — never merged into one bar — because that separation is the point
  the beat is making.
- Silent video: no narration, no background music, no sound effects.

## Notes

**The three features are fixed, and each is grounded in the codebase:**

1. **Smart Material Estimator** — turns a work scope into an exact material
   demand; keeps stock on the shelf strictly separate from stock still on
   order; two mirrored calculation engines proven identical across 1,313
   comparisons.
2. **Asset & Tool Geolocation** — serialised assets with a full custody and
   location history, GPS-tagged on movement; the Rack Locator lets you scan a
   rack and see everything that should be on it.
3. **Enforced role-based access** — permission checked centrally rather than
   screen by screen; view-only accounts are refused on 126 of 143
   data-changing actions.

**Tone constraints.** Short declarative sentences. No exclamation marks. Do not
use "revolutionary", "game-changing", "seamless", "unleash", or any similar
marketing intensifier. Do not invent statistics — the only numbers permitted are
1,313 · 126 · 143 · v1.2.0.

**Structure.** Five beats: intro title card, three feature beats, and a closing
call-to-action lockup that resolves to "GI HUB ERP · v1.2.0 — Available now"
over black, with the line "General Industries · Multi-site inventory,
procurement and planning".
